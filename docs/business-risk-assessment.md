# Business risk assessment — fictional scenario

**Question:** Which weaknesses might make a suspicious authentication sequence consequential to the business?

**Observed in synthetic records:** An alerted finance application account has MFA disabled and an overdue privileged-access review. Another inactive account retains enabled access. These are training findings, not observations about any real employer.

**Potential impact:** unauthorized access could expose confidential information, disrupt operations, or increase audit concerns. Impact and likelihood cannot be quantified from these demo inputs.

**Suggested owners:** security operations to investigate the alert; identity owner to review MFA and privileges; application owner to validate access needs.

**Proposed management measures:** MFA coverage, privileged review completion, dormant-account remediation rate, time to triage, and evidence completeness.

**Decision authority:** designated human analyst and control owner.
